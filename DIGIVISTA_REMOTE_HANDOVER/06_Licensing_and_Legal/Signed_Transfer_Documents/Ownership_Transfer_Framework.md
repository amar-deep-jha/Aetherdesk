# Digivista Remote — Intellectual Property & Ownership Transfer Framework

**Transferor:** Outgoing Project Architecture & Engineering Team  
**Transferee:** Digivista Infotech  
**Transferred Asset:** Digivista Remote (Engine codebase: AetherDesk Core v1.1.0)  
**Date:** 18 November 2026  

---

## 1. Scope of Transferred Assets
Subject to the terms of the applicable sale agreement and intellectual property assignment deeds:
1. **Source Code & Technical Assets:** Exclusive ownership of all source code, algorithms, protocol specifications, database schemas, scripts, and documentation residing in the delivered repository.
2. **Release Artifacts:** Complete authority over compiled executables, Docker containers, web templates, and release distribution archives.
3. **Operational Sovereignty:** Immediate transfer of operational responsibility to Digivista Infotech's organization-owned accounts, repositories, and cloud environments.

---

## 2. Branding & Identity Transition
- **Product Name:** The software shall henceforth be designated and marketed by the receiving organization as **Digivista Remote**.
- **Visual Branding:** Digivista Infotech shall replace transitional branding assets with its proprietary corporate logos, colors, and marketing copy.
- **Support & Domains:** Digivista Infotech shall establish its own support endpoints, privacy statements, and authoritative domain names.

---

## 3. Discontinuation of Prior Access
Upon execution of the formal handover acknowledgement:
- Prior individual developer access to production infrastructure shall be terminated.
- Digivista Infotech shall rotate all production secrets and establish its own independent deployments.
