# AETHERDESK TERMS OF SERVICE & USE
**Document Status:** Working Draft  
**Applicability:** Public Signaling, Relay Infrastructure & Application Usage  

---

## 1. Acceptance of Terms
By downloading, running, or connecting to AetherDesk signaling and relay services, you agree to comply with these Terms of Use and all relevant local telecommunications regulations.

## 2. Permitted Network Conduct
- You may utilize the public signaling server to discover and connect to peer machines with mutual consent.
- You must not overwhelm, denial-of-service (DoS), or flood the signaling or relay endpoints.
- Automated scraping or unauthorized registration of device ranges is forbidden.

## 3. Account & Unattended Access Security
- When configuring Unattended Access, users are solely responsible for setting strong, unique passwords.
- AetherDesk uses salted PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations to verify passwords locally on the host device. The project maintainers have no ability to recover forgotten unattended access credentials.

## 4. Service Availability & Modifications
The public Railway relay service is provided on a best-effort basis without warranty of uninterrupted availability. The project contributors reserve the right to apply security updates and rate limits to maintain network reliability.
