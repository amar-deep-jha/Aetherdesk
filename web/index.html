Connect in 3 Easy Steps

No complex registration, zero account credentials, and zero port-forwarding required.

1

Download & Extract

Unzip AetherDesk-Windows-x64-v1.1.0.zip on both your computer and your partner's computer.

2

Share 9-Digit Device ID

Launch AetherDesk.Client.exe. Your 9-digit Device ID (e.g., 395 253 222) will appear instantly on screen.

3

Connect & Operate

Enter your partner's ID, click Connect, and have them accept the prompt. You are now remotely controlling their PC with full 60 FPS fluidity.
